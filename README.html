⚡ Core Insight

Final-year engineering students in Tier 2/3 colleges convert on peer trust, not ads. A batchmate's WhatsApp message outperforms an Instagram ad by 5–10× for this audience. The entire strategy is built around activating peer networks and making sharing the default next step after registration — not buying reach.

  • ✅ Full registration form with client-side validation (name, phone, email, college, branch)
