Slide 1 of 5 — Target Audience
Who We're Targeting & Why They'll Register
1

🎯 Who They Are

  • B.Tech / B.E. final year (2025 batch)
  • Streams: CSE, IT, ECE (priority order)
  • Tier 2 / Tier 3 colleges — AP, TS, KA, TN, MH
  • Placement season actively underway
  • Tight-knit WhatsApp group culture

😰 Their Fear Right Now

  • "Everyone talks AI but I've never built anything"
  • "I'll freeze if they ask me about AI in the interview"
  • "My resume has no projects that stand out"
  • FOMO: batchmates already attending workshops
  • Low trust in 2-week paid courses right now

✅ Why They Register

  • Free = zero risk, zero commitment
  • 60 minutes = fits in one evening
  • "Build something" = tangible output
  • Certificate = something for the resume
  • Friend is going = social validation

📱 Where They Actually Are

  • WhatsApp (primary communication layer)
  • Instagram (passive browsing)
  • NOT LinkedIn (not a daily habit yet)
  • College placement WhatsApp groups
  • Hostel / friend group chats
⚡ Core Insight

This audience converts on peer trust, not ads. A message from a batchmate in a WhatsApp group outperforms an Instagram ad by 5–10×. The entire campaign is built around activating peer networks, not buying reach.

Slide 4 of 5 — Execution Timeline
7 Days. Every Day Has a Job.
4
  • Day 1

    🚀 Launch

    Landing page + referral system live. Identify 50 student connectors (NxtWave contacts, alumni, placement reps). Brief each connector with their personal link.

  • Day 2

    📣 First Wave Activation

    All 50 connectors post the first WhatsApp template. Email sent to 30 placement cells. First Instagram reel published.

  • Day 3

    📊 Momentum Push

    Share leaderboard screenshot in groups ("look who's leading — can you beat them?"). Follow up with non-responding connectors. Target: 200 registered.

  • Day 4

    ⚡ Mid-Campaign Check

    If <200 registered → activate ₹400 buffer on Instagram + identify 10 more WhatsApp groups. If on track → maintain momentum, post social proof.

  • Day 5

    🎬 Second Content Push

    Second Instagram reel ("48 hours left, 400+ already in"). Re-engage top referrers with current leaderboard standings.

  • Day 6

    🔥 Final Push

    "Last day" urgency message in all groups. Send to NxtWave alumni network (in-app or email). Seats counter should show single digits.

  • Day 7

    🎯 Close

    "Last 6 hours" message. Close registrations. Send confirmation + Zoom link to all registrants. Announce referral prize winners.
