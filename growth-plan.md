# NxtWave Growth Plan
## "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes" — Free Workshop
### Target: 500 Final-Year Engineering Student Registrations in 7 Days

---

## 1. WHO WE'RE TARGETING

**Primary Persona: The Anxious Final-Year Engineer**
- B.Tech / B.E. final year (2025 batch)
- Streams: CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, Mech (CSE/IT = 60% of effort)
- Tier 2 / Tier 3 colleges across AP, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra
- Placement season is live — they're scared about gaps in their resume
- They've heard "AI" everywhere but never built anything
- They want something they can show in an interview

**Why They Would Register:**
- Free = zero risk
- "60 minutes" = low time commitment
- "First AI Project" = beginner-safe, no gatekeeping
- NxtWave brand = they already trust it (or know someone who does)

**What Makes Them Register (Triggers):**
1. Peer sees value and shares it ("my friend is doing this")
2. Fear of falling behind classmates
3. A concrete promise: "You'll walk away with a project on your resume"
4. Social proof: "2,000 students already registered"

---

## 2. MARKETING CAMPAIGN PLAN

### The Core Insight
**Don't advertise. Activate peer networks.**
Final-year students trust batchmates, seniors, and WhatsApp groups — not ads.

### Channel Strategy (Prioritised, Not 20 Ideas)

---

### CHANNEL 1: WhatsApp Groups (Target: 250 registrations)
**Why:** Every engineering college has 5–15 active final-year WhatsApp groups (class groups, placement groups, coding clubs). A single message in the right group hits 60–200 people at once.

**How:**
- Identify 1–2 "connectors" per college (placement coordinators, coding club leads, class reps) — NxtWave likely already has alumni/student contacts here
- Give each connector a **personal referral link** (tracked)
- Message is short, peer-written, not corporate-sounding (see template in outreach doc)
- Target: 50 WhatsApp groups × avg 5 registrations per group = **250 registrations**

**Budget Allocation: ₹0** (pure organic activation)

---

### CHANNEL 2: Referral Engine (Target: 150 registrations)
**Why:** Students who registered will refer if there's a small incentive.

**How:**
- Every registrant gets a unique referral link on the confirmation page
- Top 3 referrers win: NxtWave Pro subscription (1 month) OR Amazon voucher (₹200 each)
- Leaderboard shown live on landing page → creates competition
- Expected: 500 initial registrants × 30% share rate × 1 referral each = **150 additional**

**Budget Allocation: ₹600** (3 × ₹200 vouchers)

---

### CHANNEL 3: College Tech Clubs & Placement Cells (Target: 60 registrations)
**Why:** Placement cells have mass email lists. Tech clubs (GDG, IEEE, ACM chapters) send weekly digests.

**How:**
- Email template sent to placement officers at 30 targeted colleges
- Request them to forward to final-year students as a "free opportunity"
- Frame it as a resource for placement prep, not an ad
- Target: 30 colleges × 2 registrations avg = **60 registrations**

**Budget Allocation: ₹0**

---

### CHANNEL 4: Instagram Reels + Stories (Target: 40 registrations)
**Why:** Final-year students are on Instagram. Short reels (30s) showing "what you'll build" drive curiosity.

**What:**
- 2 reels: one showing a before/after ("before: no AI project, after: deployed app in 60 mins")
- Story countdown with swipe-up link
- Run as paid story ad targeting: 18–22, India, interests: engineering, coding, placement

**Budget Allocation: ₹1,000** (₹500/day × 2 days of paid stories)
- Target: 40 registrations from paid + organic combined

---

### CHANNEL 5: NxtWave Alumni Network (Target: Already-Registered Students) (Bonus: 30–50 organic)
**Why:** NxtWave has existing students. An in-app notification or email to current students asking them to share takes 0 budget.

**Budget Allocation: ₹0** (internal CRM email)

## 5. WHY THIS WORKS

1. **Peer trust beats ads** — WhatsApp messages from a batchmate convert 5–10× better than Instagram ads for this audience
2. **Referral creates compounding growth** — each new registrant becomes a potential marketer
3. **Low friction** — free event, short duration, clear outcome = minimal reason to say no
4. **Right message, right fear** — "build something for your resume" speaks directly to placement anxiety
