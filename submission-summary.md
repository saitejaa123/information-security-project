## The Working Asset

**File:** `landing-page/index.html` — open directly in any browser.

**What it does:**
- Full registration form with validation
- Auto-generates a unique referral code for each registrant on submit
- Pre-fills WhatsApp share message with their personal referral link (one tap to share)
- Live countdown timer to workshop date
- Live leaderboard showing top referrers (drives competition)
- Seats counter that ticks down (real scarcity)
- Registration counter that ticks up
- Referral code auto-detected from URL `?ref=CODE` → pre-fills form + shows referred-by banner
- Mobile responsive

**Why this is the right asset to build:**
The landing page is not the growth mechanism — the referral system inside it is. Every registrant becomes a potential recruiter the moment they see their personal link and the leaderboard. That's the mechanic that compounds early registrations into 500.
